title: Promotion isn't applying at checkout
schema_ref: "topics:troubleshooting-issue"
---

A promotion that should apply doesn't show up at checkout. The
promotion's expiry date may have passed, or the order may not meet
its minimum purchase threshold — confirm both with the assistant
before assuming something is broken.
