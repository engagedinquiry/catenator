# Setting up your first promotion

As an example: {{promotion_name}} is set up as 20% off all orders
over $50, storewide, valid through August 31, non-stackable with
other promotions, applied automatically at checkout. You could
create this by telling the assistant: "Give everyone 20% off orders
over $50, storewide, through the end of August, and don't let it
combine with other discounts."
