# Introduction

{{product_name}} lets store owners manage promotions by describing
what they want in plain language to an AI assistant, instead of
clicking through a promotions dashboard. This guide is written for
store owners and team members managing an online store's promotions.

Store owners previously had to configure every promotion manually
through a dashboard form. This guide covers creating, checking, and
reviewing promotions via the assistant — not billing or storefront
design.
